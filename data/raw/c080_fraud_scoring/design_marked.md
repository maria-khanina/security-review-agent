# fraud scoring, stream of thought v2

Ok so every payment becomes an event on the MSK topic, the payments stream or the event bus depending on the diagram, and the scoring-engine, the scorer, picks it up, runs rules and model and writes the decision to the decisions db, which some docs call the scoring db, and also writes an audit record to the scoring audit log group, and that is the whole thing, three arrows, and I will say once more that the stack is all terraform so the files are the truth: kms scoring_key, msk payment_events, rds decisions_db, security group decisions_sg, log group scoring_audit and iam scoring_policy (TBD whether the policy needs a second statement for the model bucket, I think yes).

On access, I will try to be explicit this time because last review nobody could tell me how the engine is called: ⟦S:AUTH-02|calls from other internal services to the scoring-engine are authenticated with mutual TLS or signed workload identity tokens⟧ and where tokens are involved ⟦S:AUTH-03|access tokens expire within 15 minutes and refresh tokens are rotated on use⟧, and for humans, with three roles, analyst, operator, auditor, and, which the DBA team asked for twice. About what is stored where, the decision rows live in the database and the audit records live in the log group and the model files live in a bucket and nothing else is stored anywhere, and about protection, ⟦F1|data at rest is encrypted with KMS-managed keys⟧ and and ⟦S:CRYPTO-04|traffic between services, and between services and data stores or brokers, is encrypted in transit⟧ and ⟦S:CRYPTO-07|services validate TLS certificates and never disable verification⟧ and ⟦S:CRYPTO-05|only approved cryptographic algorithms are used and MD5, SHA-1, DES, 3DES and ECB mode are prohibited⟧, so that is crypto.
